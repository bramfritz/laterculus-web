Privacy Policy — Laterculus

Last updated: 23 August 2026

Laterculus (“the app”) is a language-learning app developed by Bram Fritz (“we”, “us”). This policy explains what data the app handles and why. We designed Laterculus to keep your data with you — most of it never reaches us at all.

Summary
  • We do not use third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking.
  • Your learning data (preferences, custom lists, hidden tiles) is stored in your own iCloud account, which we cannot read.
  • We only receive data when you choose to send it — by flagging a tile.
  • Payments are handled entirely by Apple; we never see your card or payment details.

What data the app handles

1. Your learning data (stored in your iCloud — not accessible to us)

Your preferences, chosen languages and level, custom lists, hidden tiles, and style settings are stored on your device and in your private iCloud (Apple’s iCloud Key-Value Storage and private CloudKit database), so they sync across your devices.

This data is tied to the Apple ID signed into your device. It lives in your iCloud account; we have no access to it. Its handling by Apple is governed by Apple’s Privacy Policy.

2. Sign in with Apple (optional)

If you choose to sign in with Apple, the app stores the identifier Apple returns, and your name and email only if you choose to share them, on your device. You can use the core of the app without signing in.

3. Tile flag reports (only when you send one)

If you tap the flag button on a tile to report a problem, the app sends the following to our CloudKit database so we can fix the content:

  • the tile’s text (word, sentence, translation, pronunciation, note);
  • the category, your selected learning and translation languages, and level;
  • the issue type and any note you add; and
  • basic diagnostics: app version, iOS version, and device model (e.g. “iPhone16,2”).

These reports do not include your name, email, Apple ID, or any advertising identifier. We use them solely to correct and improve the app’s content.

4. Subscriptions and purchases

Laterculus Pro is an auto-renewing subscription sold through Apple. All payment processing is handled by Apple; we never receive your payment information. Apple provides us only anonymized, aggregate sales information. Your purchase entitlement is verified on-device via Apple’s StoreKit.

How we use data

We use flag reports only to fix and improve the app’s learning content. We do not sell or share any data with third parties, and we do not use it for advertising.

Data retention

  • Data in your iCloud is retained and controlled by you; delete it by removing the app’s data from iCloud or signing out of iCloud.
  • Flag reports are retained only as long as needed to address the reported issue.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (including under the EU GDPR), you may have the right to access, correct, or delete data we hold about you. Because flag reports contain no identifiers, we may be unable to link a report back to you; if you can identify a report you submitted, contact us and we will delete it.

Children

Laterculus is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from them.

Changes

We may update this policy; the “Last updated” date above will change accordingly.
