Privacy Policy — Laterculus

Last updated: 26 September 2026

Laterculus (“the app”) is a language-learning app developed by Bram Fritz (“we”, “us”). This policy explains what data the app handles and why. We designed Laterculus to keep your data with you — most of it never reaches us at all.

Summary
  • We do not use third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking.
  • Your learning data (preferences, custom lists, hidden tiles) is stored in your own iCloud account, which we cannot read.
  • We only receive data when you choose to send it — by flagging a tile or requesting a feature.
  • If you use Language Buddy, a small shared session (level, word list, position) is shared through iCloud with your buddy only — not with us.
  • Payments are handled entirely by Apple; we never see your card or payment details.

What data the app handles

1. Your learning data (stored in your iCloud — not accessible to us)

Your preferences, chosen languages and level, custom lists, hidden tiles, and style settings are stored on your device and in your private iCloud (Apple’s iCloud Key-Value Storage and private CloudKit database), so they sync across your devices.

This data is tied to the Apple Account signed into your device. It lives in your iCloud account; we have no access to it. Its handling by Apple is governed by Apple’s Privacy Policy.

The app has no accounts and no sign-in of its own: it uses the iCloud account your device is already signed into.

2. Language Buddy (optional, Pro)

Language Buddy lets two people see the same word at the same time, each in their own languages. It only runs if you invite someone or accept an invite.

How it works. When you invite a buddy, the app creates a shared session in your private iCloud and shares it using Apple’s iCloud sharing. The invite is a link you send yourself (for example by Messages or AirDrop). When your buddy accepts it, the session is shared between the two of you. We never receive or see it.

What is shared with your buddy:

  • the shared level, learning order, and how often a new word appears;
  • the list of words at that level (as internal word IDs) and your shared position in it, which moves when either of you skips a word;
  • the time zone of the person who created the invite (so both phones change words at the same moment), and when the invite expires; and
  • your name, as it appears in your Apple Account, if iCloud sharing makes it available. iCloud sharing may also show your buddy other contact details Apple associates with sharing, under Apple’s rules.

What is not shared: your learning and translation languages, hidden tiles, custom lists, personal settings, and who skipped a word or when. Skips are silent: your buddy simply sees the word change.

Invite links. An invite link works once and expires after 12 hours. Until it is accepted or expires, anyone who has the link could accept it, so only send it to the person you mean to invite.

Updates between phones. To keep both phones in step, the app uses Apple’s push service (APNs) to send silent updates. These never show a notification, badge, or sound.

Ending it. You can leave at any time in Settings ▸ Language Buddy. If the person who created the invite ends the session, it is deleted from their iCloud; if the other person leaves, they are removed from the share. Either way, both phones go back to their own words and settings.

3. Tile flag reports (only when you send one)

If you tap the flag button on a tile to report a problem, the app sends the following to our CloudKit database so we can fix the content:

  • the tile’s text (word, sentence, translation, pronunciation, note);
  • the category, your selected learning and translation languages, and level;
  • the issue type and any note you add; and
  • basic diagnostics: app version, iOS version, and device model (e.g. “iPhone16,2”).

4. Feature requests (only when you send one)

If you use Request a Feature (Pro), the app sends your note, your selected learning and translation languages and level, and the same basic diagnostics (app version, iOS version, device model) to our CloudKit database.

Flag reports and feature requests do not include your name, email, Apple Account, or any advertising identifier. We use them solely to improve the app.

5. Subscriptions and purchases

Laterculus Pro is an auto-renewing subscription sold through Apple. All payment processing is handled by Apple; we never receive your payment information. Apple provides us only anonymized, aggregate sales information. Your purchase entitlement is verified on-device via Apple’s StoreKit.

How we use data

We use flag reports and feature requests only to fix and improve the app. We do not sell or share any data with third parties, and we do not use it for advertising.

Data retention

  • Data in your iCloud is retained and controlled by you; delete it by removing the app’s data from iCloud or signing out of iCloud.
  • A Language Buddy session exists only while it’s active: it is deleted when the person who created it ends it, and an unused invite is deleted the next time the inviter opens the app after it expires (an expired link can no longer be accepted either way).
  • Flag reports and feature requests are retained only as long as needed to act on them.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (including under the EU GDPR), you may have the right to access, correct, or delete data we hold about you. Because flag reports and feature requests contain no identifiers, we may be unable to link one back to you; if you can identify one you submitted, contact us and we will delete it. Language Buddy data is stored in iCloud, not with us: you control it by leaving or ending the session.

Children

Laterculus is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from them.
