Adding the widget

  1. On your Home Screen, touch and hold an empty area until the apps jiggle.
  2. Tap Edit in the top-left corner, then Add Widget.
  3. Search for Laterculus, pick a size and tap Add Widget.

For the Lock Screen: touch and hold the Lock Screen, tap Customize, choose the Lock Screen, and add Laterculus to the widget area.

Laterculus Pro

  • Start or manage your subscription: in the app, go to Settings ▸ Become Pro. To cancel, open Settings on your iPhone ▸ your name ▸ Subscriptions ▸ Laterculus Pro.
  • Pro not showing up? In the app, go to Settings ▸ Become Pro ▸ Restore Purchases, signed in with the same Apple Account you subscribed with.
  • Refunds are handled by Apple: reportaproblem.apple.com.

Language Buddy

  • Both of you need Laterculus Pro and need to be signed in to iCloud.
  • To invite someone: Settings ▸ Language Buddy ▸ Invite a Buddy, and send the link. They tap it and choose Accept. Make sure Laterculus is installed on their iPhone first.
  • An invite link works once and expires after 12 hours. If it has expired, create a new one.
  • To stop: Settings ▸ Language Buddy ▸ Leave Buddy. You'll both go back to your own words and settings.
